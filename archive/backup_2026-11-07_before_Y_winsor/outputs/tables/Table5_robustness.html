Two-way fixed-effects (firm and year) panel regressions, same estimator and sample as Table 4 (N = 219 firm-years, 24 firms; 7780 and 7791 have one observation each and are absorbed by the firm effect).
Standard errors in parentheses are clustered by firm, CR1 (G/(G-1) x (N-1)/(N-K)). Stars use two-sided p-values from a t distribution with G-1 = 21 degrees of freedom, G = firms with at least two observations (Cameron and Miller 2015): *** p<0.01, ** p<0.05, * p<0.1.
Wild cluster bootstrap p-value for the financial-constraint (FC) variable of each column: restricted (null imposed), Rademacher weights, B = 9999, seed = 20261106, on the equivalent least-squares-dummy-variable regression, clusters = firms.
Regressors are winsorized at the 1st/99th percentiles of the 219-row sample; SA is computed from winsorized Size and Age. Dependent variables are used as exported by TEJ (not winsorized, D18).
Column (1) repeats Table 4 column (1).
Column (2), brief §5.1: SA dummy = 1 if the firm-year's SA Index is above the sample median (-4.7612, pooled 219 firm-years), 0 otherwise; 109 firm-years equal 1. With firm fixed effects the coefficient is identified only from the 5 firms whose dummy changes over time (1215, 1217, 1220, 1231, 1235).
Column (3), brief §5.3: SA Index lagged one year within firm (previous row must be year t-1). For 2016 the lag is SA(2015), computed from 2015 total assets, FX rate and founding year with the same winsorizing cutoffs (2015 used as t-1 input only). For the two singleton firms (7780, 7791) the lag is SA(2024) from their pre-listing year; they do not affect the coefficients.
Column (4), brief §5.4: Model 2 adds SIZE = ln(total assets, NTD thousand), winsorized. SIZE and SA are mechanically related (correlation -0.586 pooled, -0.827 after removing firm and year means); VIF in the second sheet: pooled (OLS with constant, as in Table 3) and on the two-way-demeaned regressors, which is the collinearity the FE estimator faces.
