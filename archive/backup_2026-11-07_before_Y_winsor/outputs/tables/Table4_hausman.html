FE: firm fixed effects plus year dummies; RE: random firm effects plus year dummies; both with the conventional (non-robust) covariance, as the classic Hausman test requires. H = b'(V_FE - V_RE)^-1 b over the six slopes, df = 6.
Mundlak: random-effects regression adding the firm means of the six regressors, firm-clustered; Wald test that the six mean coefficients are jointly zero. Rejection means the firm effects are correlated with the regressors (FE preferred).
Decision rule from brief §3.2: p < 0.05 -> fixed effects. The main model is the two-way FE of brief §3.3.
